MEET THE FOUNDER

Meet Asna — Founder of ReachX Infotech

ReachX Infotech is an IT and Digital Training institute built with one clear purpose—to make learning practical, structured, and relevant for today’s fresher job market. Our mission is to support freshers who feel stuck after learning basics and don’t know how to convert knowledge into real, usable skills. We focus on a job-ready training system that avoids theory overload and emphasizes hands-on learning through a clear 180-day roadmap, so learners can steadily build Python Full-Stack development skills with clarity and direction.

ReachX Infotech was created to bridge the gap between learning and real-world application. Asna’s mission is to guide freshers, graduates, and early career aspirants who want a practical path into Python Full-Stack development but feel overwhelmed by scattered courses and unclear guidance. Through structured learning, real project exposure, and a supportive environment, ReachX Infotech is designed to help learners move forward with focus, consistency, and purpose.
