MEET THE FOUNDER

Meet Asna — Founder of ReachX Infotech

ReachX Infotech is an IT and Digital Skills Training Institute focused on helping students, graduates, and freshers build practical, industry relevant skills and move towards their career goals. ReachX Infotech provides structured learning, hands on practice, real project exposure, internships, and career guidance.

Our goal is to bridge the gap between academic education and real world industry requirements. Instead of focusing only on theory, we help learners develop practical skills that they can apply through projects, portfolio building, internships, interview preparation, and career focused training.

ReachX Infotech offers career focused programs in Data Science, Data Analytics, AI & Machine Learning, Full Stack Development, and Digital Marketing. Each program is designed to help students understand the fundamentals, practise their skills, work on real projects, and build confidence for their next career step.
